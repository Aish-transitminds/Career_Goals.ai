Rejection Recovery Roadmap

Turn admission setbacks into deterministic recovery strategies.

1. Identify Primary Diagnostic Cause

⚠ ECTS / Credit Deficiency

Undergraduate syllabus lacked theoretical or higher mathematics credits (common in Germany research universities).

⚑ Test Score Gap

IELTS sub-band below 6.0 or GRE Quant below 75th percentile cutoffs.

⏱ Capacity & Quota Exhaustion

Applied close to deadline when non-EU seat caps were already fully allocated.

✍ Statement Alignment Deficit

Motivation letter lacked specific curricular research links or module anchors.

Recommended Strategic Recovery Plan

Target Irish universities or German Fachhochschulen (Universities of Applied Sciences) that evaluate holistic practical competencies rather than rigid theoretical credit matrices.
